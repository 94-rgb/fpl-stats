How these numbers are made

Everything is downloaded and calculated in your browser when the page opens, so it updates as soon as the dataset does, usually within a day of a gameweek finishing. Price, position, minutes, starts, xG, xA, xGI, xGC, defensive contributions, saves, cards, bonus, BPS, points, goals, assists and clean sheets come from the official FPL API, via the FPL-Core-Insights dataset on GitHub. xGOT is summed from that dataset's match-level player stats.

npxG removes 0.79 xG for every penalty a player has taken, scored or missed, which is the value the underlying provider assigns to a penalty. npxGI is npxG plus xA.

Team xCS is an estimate: for each match, the chance of a clean sheet is taken as e−xG using the opponent's xG, and those chances are added up. Its per-90 figure is per team match. It is shown against a player's current club.

Hit rates count matches where a player reached 60 minutes, reached the DEFCON threshold (10 for defenders, 12 for midfielders and forwards; goalkeepers can't earn it) or made 3 or more saves. "Appearances" divides by matches the player got on the pitch; "Team matches" divides by every match their club has played. A double gameweek counts as two matches and a blank gameweek as none. Single gameweeks use the official FPL figures; double gameweeks are split using match-level stats, which agree with FPL's DEFCON threshold calls on every match checked so far.

Last non-start is the most recent gameweek in which a player's club played a match he didn't start, with the minutes he played that gameweek beside it. It counts being benched, being an unused substitute, and being out of the squad altogether, so read it as rotation and availability risk rather than benchings alone. "Never" means he has started every match his club has played. Single gameweeks use the official FPL starts figure; doubles fall back to match-level data where that shows more starts.

Shortlists and your filter settings are saved in this browser, so they won't appear on another device or if you clear site data.

Total points always shows the season total, so it stays put when the table is in Per 90 mode and the Points column becomes points per 90. In Totals mode the two columns match; hide either one from Columns if you don't want both.

Save current stores everything above the table — the builder, the position, team, price and minutes controls, the Totals or Per 90 choice, the hit-rate basis, and the sort order — under a name you give it. Picking a saved filter puts all of that back. "Edited since saving" means the current view no longer matches what's stored; Update overwrites it, or save it under a new name to keep both. Saved filters live in this browser alongside your shortlists.

The filter builder combines conditions with and/or. A group set to "Match all of" needs every condition in it to be true; "Match any of" needs just one. Add a nested group to mix the two, for example match all of [price at most 5.5] and [any of: DEFCON hit at least 50%, xGI per 90 at least 0.3]. Position and team are available as conditions too, so you can write rules that apply to one position only. An empty condition is ignored rather than matching nothing.

Per-90 figures are only shown for players with minutes. Stat filters apply to whatever is on screen, so a filter set in Per 90 mode filters per-90 values.
